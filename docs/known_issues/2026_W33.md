## 34 documents were ingested with the default legal area and were never re-ingested

> **Live** · this is **G-3**, inherited from the 2026-08-07 classifier failure
> **Detected:** 2026-08-15 · **Last verified:** 2026-08-15 against `ddf1d5d` — not re-checked against the current corpus
> Full evidence: `git show 3792b14:docs/registro/2026-08-15/cambio-modelos-groq.md`

On 2026-08-07 the legal-area classifier was failing silently: Groq answered 400 in
bursts and an empty `catch` swallowed it, so **the whole corpus fell to "Derecho
administrativo"** — which is exactly the field the platform filters by. The cause
was fixed; the documents already ingested with the wrong area were not.

34 documents are affected. They are not broken data in an obvious way: they carry a
plausible area, they just carry the wrong one, and nothing in the corpus marks them
apart from correctly classified ones.

Two things need re-checking before acting, and neither has been done: whether those
34 are still in the corpus after the reset of 2026-08-24 (see the assistant's
`known_issues/`), and whether the ledger can still identify them.

## `LLM_MODEL` on the VM was never confirmed after the Groq shutdown

> **Unverifiable from the repo** · this is **G-2**
> **Detected:** 2026-08-15 · **Last verified:** 2026-08-15 against `ddf1d5d` · the shutdown date (2026-08-16) has passed

The three defaults in this repo were moved to `openai/gpt-oss-20b` a day before
Groq shut down `llama-3.1-8b-instant` and `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` — verified
today, `src/services/llm/index.ts:87` and `src/modules/spij/services/llm/index.ts:79,138`.

But the campaign VM sets `LLM_MODEL` explicitly, and **an explicit variable beats
the new default**. If it still points at a shut-down model, area classification has
been failing since 2026-08-16, and it fails the way that hurts: the area falls back
to the default **with a warning in the ledger**, so ingestion keeps running and the
corpus keeps degrading quietly.

It cannot be checked from here. It takes someone with access to the VM reading its
`.env`. Until then, treat "the model problem is fixed" as true of the code and
unknown of the machine.

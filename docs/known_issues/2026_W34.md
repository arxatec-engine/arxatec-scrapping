## gob.pe publishes the same resolution under two URLs, and both are ingested

> **Live** · this is **DU-1/DU-2** · found from `arxatec-lawyer-service` while building a retrieval golden set
> **Detected:** 2026-08-20 · **Last verified:** 2026-08-20 against `main` plus a real TFL ingestion and a query against the assistant's PostgreSQL
> Full evidence: `git show 3792b14:docs/registro/2026-08-20/DUPLICADO_POR_URL.md`

In 24 ingested Tribunal de Fiscalización Laboral documents, two share a number:

```sql
select document_number, count(*) from documents
group by document_number having count(*) > 1;
-- 005-2023-SUNAFIL-TFL | 2
```

It is not an ingestion bug. gob.pe publishes the same resolution under two
addresses (`…/3914769-005-2023` and `…/3914799-005-2023`), and document identity is
derived from `source_url` — a deliberate decision, documented in
`src/services/ingest-local/ids.ts:7-8`, that solves the frequent problem
(re-ingesting without duplicating) and does not cover the inverse one.

What it costs at scale:

- **Retrieval**: the two documents compete for the same `top_k`, so one resolution
  eats two slots.
- **Embedding cost**: paid twice.
- **Citations**: the assistant can cite either, and they are not the same URL.

Two things are open, and the first has to happen before the second: measure how
many repeated numbers there are per source once the corpus grows (that is work →
`../BACKLOG.md`), then decide the identity policy — owner's call. With one
observation there is no way to tell an isolated case from gob.pe's normal
behaviour.

Related, and the reason this is not simply "the backend should dedup": see
[`2026_W30.md`](2026_W30.md).
